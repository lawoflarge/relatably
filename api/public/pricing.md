# Relatably Pricing

Relatably is free to download and play on iPhone.

- **Price:** Free (USD 0.00)
- **Subscription:** None
- **Paywall:** None
- **In-app purchases:** None
- **Platform:** iOS 17.0 or later (iPhone)
- **App Store:** https://apps.apple.com/us/app/relatably/id6768082631

Every core feature — the daily question, typing your free-text answer, and viewing the global live wordcloud — is available at no cost. Signing in with Apple to compete on the global leaderboards and friend other users is also free.
